# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 8, 2026**

Déjà Entendu is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains
exactly what that means.

## Summary

Déjà Entendu does not have a server, does not create accounts, does not run
ads, and does not use any analytics or tracking software. Everything you
import — a recording, a photo, a PDF, or typed text — is processed and
stored only on your phone.

## What the app does with your data

- **Recordings, photos, PDFs, and text you import.** These are copied into
  the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded
  anywhere. Transcription, translation, study-note generation, and
  pronunciation guides all run using Gemini Nano, Google's on-device AI
  model, directly on your phone — your recordings and text are never sent
  to Déjà Entendu, to Google, or to any other third party for this
  processing.
- **Vocabulary lists and settings.** Stored locally on your device, the
  same way. Deleting the app deletes this data.
- **Microphone access.** Used only when you choose to record audio in the
  app (either importing an existing file or using "Record Live"), and to
  run on-device transcription on that audio. The app does not record or
  listen in the background, and audio never leaves your device.
- **Internet access.** The app requests internet permission solely so
  Android's on-device AI system (Google AI Core / Play Services) can
  download the Gemini Nano model components the first time a device uses
  them. Déjà Entendu itself has no server and does not transmit your
  content over the internet.

## What the app does not do

- No account creation or sign-in
- No advertising or ad networks
- No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs
- No sale or sharing of your data with third parties
- No cloud backup of your samples, recordings, or vocabulary (standard
  Android device backup, if you have it enabled system-wide, is controlled
  by your device settings, not by this app)

## Third-party processing

The only component outside the app itself involved in your data is
Android's own on-device Gemini Nano model (via Google AI Core), which runs
locally on your phone. Google's handling of the on-device model runtime
itself is governed by Android's own system privacy policies, not by Déjà
Entendu — the app never sends your content to Google's servers as part of
this processing.

## Children's privacy

Déjà Entendu does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of
any age, since it does not collect information at all in the first place —
all data stays on-device as described above.
