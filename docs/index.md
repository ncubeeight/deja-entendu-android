# Déjà Entendu

An Android app for capturing spoken language — a recording, a photo, a PDF,
or typed text — and turning it into a transcript, translation, and study
notes, entirely on-device via Gemini Nano. Nothing you import or record
leaves your phone.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy.html)

For Pixel 10 and other Gemini-Nano-v3-class devices.
